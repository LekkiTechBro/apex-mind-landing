A Note for Parents

Every child learns at their own pace, and progress rarely looks like a straight line. A struggling result today is often just a signal that a child needs more guidance, more practice, or a different approach — not a verdict on who they are. Before a child is labelled "slow" or written off, it's worth asking whether they've truly been given the support, patience, and encouragement to show what they're capable of.
